Boltzmann 4D is a web version of [Boltzmann 3D](https://boltzmann3d.byu.edu/), a kinetic theory demonstrator from Brigham Young University. It runs the same event-driven hard-sphere physics in your browser, with nothing to install.

## Original program

- Author: Dr. Randall B. Shirts
- Programmers: Scott R. Burt, Benjamin J. Lemmon
- Additional programming: Jared Duke, Derek Manwaring, Aaron Stewart, Dustin Carr

Boltzmann 3D is copyright Brigham Young University (2004–2013) and Dr. Randall B. Shirts.
